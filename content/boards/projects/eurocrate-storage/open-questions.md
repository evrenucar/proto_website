# Open questions

## Anti-tip interlock

Worth building at all? The failure case is several loaded drawers open at once
pulling the whole frame over.

- Engages once a crate moves even slightly out
- Releases when the crate is removed completely, since a crate that is out of
  the frame cannot tip it
- Is "only one open at a time" the right rule, or too restrictive to live with?

## Sourcing

Second hand supply is the whole reason for standardising on 600x400. Are
Marktplaats and liquidation sales reliable enough to count on, or does emtrade
end up being the default anyway?

## Weight budget

Confirm the base ballast beats a fully loaded, fully extended set of drawers
before committing to a frame design. That number decides how much mass goes in
the base, which decides whether it can be moved at all.

## Why not just boxes

Worth answering honestly before building anything. Stacked boxes are free. The
frame only earns its place if getting to the bottom crate stops being a chore.
