# Design notes

A steel welded frame carrying eurocrates as drawers. The crates stay standard
600x400, so they remain cheap to source second hand and easy to replace.

## Sliding

- HDPE strips on the sliding surfaces
- Teflon on the friction points if HDPE alone is not slippery enough
- A thin sheet over the top surface so dust does not settle into an open crate

## Stability

- Ballast in the base, sand or klinker blocks
- The base has to outweigh the loaded drawers: 6 crates, 20kg maximum each
- Heavy duty castors, locking

## Small parts

EuroPlus flex organizers inside a crate for anything too small to sit loose.

## Sizes in use

The 600x400 footprint comes in three heights, 75mm, 170mm and 270mm. The
400x300x120 is the half-footprint for lighter things. Supplier links are on the
board next to this note.
